The affordable way up

Same hills, smaller bill

Most of the cost of a hill trip sits in four places: the car, the room, the week you travel and the number of times you move. Here is how we bring each one down without making the trip worse.

  • {% icon "jeep" %}Share the jeep on transfer daysA seat in a shared jeep costs a fraction of a reserved car on the same road. We tell you which stand, which time and which seat.
  • {% icon "calendar" %}Travel in the shoulder weeksRates fall sharply outside the Puja holidays, Christmas week and April to June. Late November and February are clear and cheaper.
  • {% icon "village" %}Sleep in a villageFamily homestays usually include dinner and breakfast and cost less than a town hotel plus meals.
  • {% icon "route" %}Move lessTwo or three bases mean fewer car days, the single biggest line in most quotes.
