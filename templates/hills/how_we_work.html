How we work

Five steps, no surprises

You pay nothing until the plan is right. Here is exactly what happens between your first message and your last transfer.

{% include "hills/partials/trust.html" %}
Step01

You tell us the tale

Dates, who is travelling, which lands interest you and roughly what you want to spend. Six questions online, a message on WhatsApp or a call. Rough answers are fine.

Step02

A first route within one working day

Your planner sends a day-by-day route with named hotels and room categories, drive times and an indicative price per person. It is a draft to react to.

Step03

We refine it together

Change the pace, swap a hotel, add a safari or a homestay night, drop a long drive. We revise until it is right, then send an itemised written quote.

Step04

You book with a deposit

A [25]% deposit confirms the booking; the balance is due [45] days before departure. We confirm every hotel, car, guide and permit in writing. See payments and refunds and cancellations.

Step05

On the road, a message away

Your planner stays with your journey while you travel. A driver meets you at arrival, and landslide detours or closed passes are re-planned while you are there.

What every journey includes

The standard, not the extras

The right vehicle for the budget

A reserved car and driver for your party, or shared jeeps on transfer days when you want to save. Pickup at Bagdogra, NJP or Siliguri.

Local drivers and guides

People who live on these roads and know which pass is open, which viewpoint is clear and where lunch is honest.

Stays at your price level

Homestays and government lodges, comfortable mid-range hotels or heritage properties, named in the quote.

Permits and paperwork

We arrange the permits a registered agent can apply for and tell you about the rest.

One planner throughout

The person who planned it is the person you message on the road.

An itemised quote

Every line priced, taxes shown, nothing hidden in a total.
