{% icon "ticket" %} Affordable trips

A cheaper trip, not a worse one

Same routes, same permits, same care. You save on the things that do not change the trip: shared jeeps on transfer days, the quieter months, homestays and government lodges, and two or three bases instead of five.

Where the money goes

Five ways we keep the price down

  • {% icon "jeep" %}
    Shared jeeps on transfer daysA seat from NJP or Siliguri to Darjeeling, Kalimpong or Gangtok costs a fraction of a reserved car. We keep the car for sightseeing days, when it earns its price.
  • {% icon "calendar" %}
    The quieter monthsLate November to early December and February give clear mountains at lower rates. Peak weeks are the Puja holidays, Christmas to New Year and April to mid-June.
  • {% icon "bed" %}
    Homestays and government lodgesVillage homestays usually include dinner and breakfast. WBTDC, forest department and Sikkim Tourism lodges sit in some of the best spots at fair prices.
  • {% icon "route" %}
    Fewer basesEvery hotel change costs half a day and a transfer. Two or three bases with day trips is cheaper and calmer than a new town every night.
  • {% icon "sparkle" %}
    What is freeMonasteries, the Mall and Chowrasta, MG Marg, tea-garden roads, village trails and most viewpoints cost nothing. We tell you which tickets are worth buying.
